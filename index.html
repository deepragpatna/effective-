Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your project with us.

How much does a website development project cost?
Website development costs depend on the scope, features, and complexity of your project. A basic business website typically starts from ₹8,000–₹25,000. E-commerce websites range from ₹25,000–₹1,00,000+. Custom web applications are priced based on requirements. We always provide a detailed, transparent quotation before starting work — no hidden fees.
How long does it take to build a mobile app?
A simple mobile app takes 4–8 weeks, while a feature-rich app with backend APIs can take 3–6 months. The timeline depends on complexity, number of screens, integrations, and the platform (Android, iOS, or both). We provide a detailed timeline during the planning phase so you always know what to expect.
What is included in your School ERP solution?
Our School ERP includes: student admissions and profiles, attendance management, fee collection and receipts, exam and result management, timetable scheduling, library management, transport tracking, parent-teacher communication portal, staff payroll, and a mobile app for parents and teachers. We can customize any module to fit your institution's specific needs.
Do you offer post-launch maintenance and support?
Yes! We offer flexible AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) packages that include bug fixes, security patches, performance monitoring, content updates, and technical support. Our support team is reachable via phone, WhatsApp, and email. We believe the relationship with our clients continues long after launch.
Will my website be mobile-friendly and SEO optimized?
Absolutely. Every website we build is fully responsive (looks great on mobile, tablet, and desktop), follows Google's Core Web Vitals, is optimized for fast loading, and includes basic on-page SEO — proper meta tags, semantic HTML, structured data, alt texts, and sitemaps. We can also provide ongoing SEO services to improve your search rankings.
What technologies and platforms do you work with?
Our tech stack spans React, Next.js, Vue.js, Node.js, Laravel, PHP, Python for web; Flutter, React Native, Kotlin, Swift for mobile; MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB for databases; AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure for cloud; and WordPress, Shopify, and WooCommerce for CMS/e-commerce platforms. We choose the right tool for your project's specific needs.
Can you redesign or upgrade my existing website or software?
Yes! We handle website redesigns, legacy software modernization, performance optimization, and feature additions. Whether it's migrating from an old CMS, upgrading to a modern tech stack, or adding new modules to an existing system — our team can assess your current solution and suggest the most efficient path forward.
Do you work with clients outside Bihar and India?
Absolutely. While we are headquartered in Bodh Gaya, Bihar, we work with clients across India and internationally. We conduct project discussions over video calls, maintain regular communication via WhatsApp and email, and use project management tools to keep remote collaboration seamless and transparent.
